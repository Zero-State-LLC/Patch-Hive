# Official maker pages count as publication for facts

The catalog is built from makers' own spec pages, and no maker gave permission for that. On 2026-11-04 the owner decided that official pages count as publication for facts only (name, HP, power, depth, category), on maker-owned domains, with robots.txt honoured and no route around a block. A maker who asks is opted out, and anything beyond facts, or a maker whose terms forbid automated access, needs permission first.

Asking all 703 brands first was the stronger basis, but replies would be partial, and the 2,492 rows already loaded would have to leave bulk publication and export while waiting. This is an engineering policy, not legal advice: counsel reads it before anyone sells or bulk-exports the catalog, which is where "publication" matters most.

## Amended 2026-11-08

The owner widened this to descriptions, manuals and pictures on a maker's own site (decision 65), on their report that most of the makers they contacted say this material is Creative Commons. Those replies aren't in this repository, and the limit to facts above no longer holds. The other conditions stand: maker-owned domains, robots.txt honoured, no route around a block, the opt-out, and permission first from a maker whose terms forbid automated access. Counsel still reads the policy before anyone sells or bulk-exports the catalog.
