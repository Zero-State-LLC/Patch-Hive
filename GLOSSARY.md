# PatchHive

A Eurorack rig-intelligence and Patch Book publishing product. It reasons about hardware people own; it doesn't process audio or control hardware.

## Language

**Rig**:
A person's modular synthesizer: the Modules they own, placed in a Case.
_Avoid_: Rack, system, setup

**Case**:
The physical enclosure that holds Modules and gives them HP and power.
_Avoid_: Rack

**HP**:
The unit of width in a Case.
_Avoid_: TE

**Module**:
A working unit that occupies HP in a Case, whether or not a Patch can use it.
_Avoid_: Device, product

**Blank Panel**:
A plate that covers empty HP. It isn't a Module, because it reserves no space.
_Avoid_: Blank module

**Module Category**:
What a Module does, such as VCO, LFO, VIDEO or SYSTEM.
_Avoid_: Category (on its own), type

**Patch Category**:
What a Patch is for, such as Voice or Generative.
_Avoid_: Category (on its own)
