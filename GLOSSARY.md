# PatchHive

A Eurorack rig-intelligence and Patch Book publishing product. It reasons about hardware people own; it doesn't process audio or control hardware.

## Language

**Rig**:
A person's modular synthesizer: the Modules they own and their Placements, in a Case once they name one.
_Avoid_: Rack, system, setup

**Case**:
The physical enclosure that holds Modules and gives them HP and power.
_Avoid_: Rack

**HP**:
The unit of width in a Case.
_Avoid_: TE

**Module**:
A working unit that occupies HP in a Case, whether or not a Patch can use it.
_Avoid_: Device, product

**Blank Panel**:
A plate that covers empty HP. It isn't a Module, because it reserves no space.
_Avoid_: Blank module

**Module Category**:
What a Module does, such as VCO, LFO, VIDEO or SYSTEM.
_Avoid_: Category (on its own), type

**Rig Photo**:
A photo a Rig owner takes of their whole Rig, or of some of its rows, to start or update the Rig.
_Avoid_: Scan, image (on its own)

**Reading**:
What PatchHive saw in a Rig Photo: a set of Sightings. A Reading stays evidence; confirming it makes the owner's chosen Modules and Positions part of the Rig. It is current until it is confirmed or replaced.
_Avoid_: Scan result, detection

**Retake**:
A new Rig Photo that replaces an earlier Reading.
_Avoid_: Rescan

**Sighting**:
One thing a Reading found in a Rig Photo, with where it sits and what it might be: a Module, a Blank Panel, something that isn't Eurorack, or something unknown.
_Avoid_: Candidate (that is one possible identity of a Sighting), box

**Position**:
Where a Module sits in a Rig: its row, and the HP where it starts.
_Avoid_: Slot

**Placement**:
A Module at a Position in a Rig.
_Avoid_: Slot

**Rig Revision**:
A fixed snapshot of a Rig's Placements and Case; every run and Patch Book is made from one.
_Avoid_: Version, snapshot (on its own)

**Patch Category**:
What a Patch is for, such as Voice or Generative.
_Avoid_: Category (on its own)
