# Intent

## Problem

Spec 003 T031 classifies the catalog rows that have no category. In a load from the committed inputs that is 702 live rows. 207 of them have maker tags that no rule matches. 425 have no tags at all, because the feed adapters drop the store's own product type, tags and categories. Catalog fetching is on hold until the owner signs off the maker-permission basis, so T031's paid re-crawl cannot run.

## Proposed outcome

An offline pass:

- whole-tag rules for the tagged rows, each spot-checked on every row it classifies, written to a fill-null overlay (`module-catalog-category-v3`, 74 rows);
- feed adapters that keep the store taxonomy (`store_tags`) for the next crawl;
- a dry run against a scratch database loaded from main;
- the remainder documented, with the two owner calls: video modules (a VIDEO category or UTIL) and power supplies (retire them or not).

Evidence: [CATALOG_T031_OFFLINE_PASS_2026-11-03.md](../docs/evidence/CATALOG_T031_OFFLINE_PASS_2026-11-03.md).

Out of scope: any fetch, any load to staging or prod, new categories, retirements, and edits to spec 003 (other sessions own it).

## Affected users / systems

Catalog inputs and the crawl scripts. `inputs_hash` changes, and the next db-load dry run plans 74 `category_overlay` updates. No schema change.

## Constraints / non-goals

Fill-null only. A row whose tags point to two categories stays empty. Video and power-supply rows are held, not guessed. No ModularGrid.

## Open questions

- Video modules: a VIDEO category or UTIL? Owner.
- Retire the 11 power-supply rows? Owner.
- Rectifier and crossfader conventions, and a reviewed table for Mutable Instruments derivatives. Owner, later.
